The problem

Vendor checks end up as a folder of invoices, screenshots and notes.

Collecting more data is not the hard part. Keeping enough provenance to answer simple questions later is.

“Is the NIF on this invoice the same one the supplier gave us — and who checked it, when, and how?”
  • Where did this RC number come from?
  • Did the invoice and the supplier record contain the same NIF?
  • Is “SARL” vs “S.A.R.L.” punctuation — or a real SARL/EURL conflict?
  • Was a field missing, or was the source itself down?
  • Was the result an exact rule or a fuzzy guess?
  • Can another reviewer reproduce it next month?

Design principles

What makes DalilDZ different

01

Evidence, not reputation

Reports evidence states. Never labels a company safe, fraudulent or trustworthy.

02

Deterministic first

RC, NIF, NIS, legal form and wilaya checks are exact rules — no AI needed.

03

Provenance survives

Source, method, document hash, page, retrieval time and snapshot hash are kept.

04

Failure is explicit

A broken source is SOURCE UNAVAILABLE, never silently “not found”.

05

History is not rewritten

A refresh creates a new analysis; earlier reports stay intact.

06

Lawful by design

No CAPTCHA bypass or scraping of official registries. Manual-only sources stay manual.

دليل · dalil = evidence, guide

Evidence and consistency — not a trust score.

Open source under Apache-2.0. Contributions welcome: Algerian data sources, Arabic/French document fixtures, normalization edge cases, accessibility.

github.com/dinogx99/DalilDZ

DalilDZ does not determine whether an entity is trustworthy, solvent, legitimate, fraudulent, or safe. It reports evidence and inconsistencies found in supplied and publicly available information. All examples use synthetic test data.
