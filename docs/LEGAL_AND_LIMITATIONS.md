# Legal and Product Limitations

DalilDZ is a software tool for organizing and comparing evidence. It is not legal, accounting, credit, solvency or fraud-detection advice.

## Prohibited product interpretations

A DalilDZ report must not be presented as:
- a guarantee that a business exists legally;
- a guarantee that a vendor will perform;
- a credit score;
- a fraud verdict;
- a safety certification;
- a global trust score.

## Source access

Contributors must respect source terms, robots policies, authentication, subscriptions, rate limits and applicable law. A manual verification adapter is acceptable and preferable to circumvention.

## Data quality

Public sources can be stale. Documents can be forged. Company-controlled websites are self-published. OCR can make mistakes. Fuzzy matching is not a legal identity determination.

The purpose of provenance is to make those limitations visible to the reviewer.
